Melisa Arukgoda
Geometric Mechanics & Robotics

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Pennsylvania State University, State College. My current research is in geometric mechanics — exploring the mathematical structures that underlie motion, control, and dynamics in physical systems. My broader background spans robotics, autonomous systems, and sensor optimization, with work spanning LiDAR placement, robot design, and human-robot interaction during my time at SUTD under Prof. Mohan Rajesh Elara.

Research Interests

Geometric Mechanics

Mathematical structures underlying motion and control — Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics on manifolds, Lie group methods, and variational principles for physical systems.

Autonomous Robotics

LiDAR sensor placement optimization, environment mapping, and perception pipelines for mobile robots operating in dynamic, real-world environments.

Computational Dynamics

Numerical methods for mechanical systems, structure-preserving integration, and simulation of constrained robotic and multibody systems.

Robot Design Optimization

Evolutionary and gradient-based approaches to sensor configuration, reconfigurable robot architectures, and scalable platform design.
