Research Assistant · Mechanical Engineering · Penn State University

Melisa Oshaini Arukgoda
Geometric Mechanics & Robotics

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Pennsylvania State University, working with Prof. Baxi Chong on bio-inspired robot locomotion — modeling, simulation, and optimization of geometric locomotion. I hold a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka. I was selected as one of 30 fellows worldwide for the MIT Summer Geometry Initiative 2025, and my broader research spans geometric mechanics, humanoid HRI and computational robot design.

Research Interests

Geometric Mechanics

Bio-inspired locomotion, modeling and simulation of geometric locomotion, optimization of motion using Lie group methods and variational principles for robotic systems.

Autonomous Robotics

sensor optimization, environment mapping, and perception pipelines for mobile and reconfigurable robots in real-world environments.

Human–Robot Interaction

Expressive humanoid systems integrating facial recognition, speech processing, and LLM-based dialogue for natural human-robot interaction.
